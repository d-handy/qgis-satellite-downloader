description=Satellite data for any place on Earth without registration: type a place, choose a purpose (current image, radar, elevation), load. Expert mode with 1267 datasets from 27 providers.
about=Searches public STAC catalogues (Microsoft Planetary Computer, INPE Brazil Data Cube, Digital Earth Africa, Element84, swisstopo and others) and bundled indexes of static catalogues for a rectangle or polygon and a date range. Cloud-optimised GeoTIFFs are read only where they overlap the area of interest (GDAL /vsicurl/), mosaicked per day, stacked and clipped. Sentinel-3 netCDF swath products are downloaded as whole files. No account or API key is needed.
